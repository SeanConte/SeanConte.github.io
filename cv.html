Manuscripts

Complete

2026
“Shifting between Modal & Social Variance: The Instrumental Value of Diversity in Hedging against the Unpredictability of Evidence.” Submitting to Philosophy of Science, November 2026.

Shows that the variance of probabilistic evidence appears either as disagreement within a community or as unpredictability across its possible outcomes, depending on how members’ evidence overlaps.

In preparation

“Wise Cascades: Information Cascades and the Wisdom of Crowds.” Extended abstract accepted at Complex Networks 2026; full paper to be submitted, December 2026.

Unifies the canonical cascade and wisdom-of-crowds models through a trust parameter, and proves that any target level of reliability can be reached by a correct cascade.

“Mereological Conflict in Scientific Communities.” To be submitted, December 2026.

Surveys and taxonomizes problems in which what raises each member’s expected accuracy lowers the community’s, relates them to existing problems in the literature, and shows that several established models contain such conflicts that have gone unnoticed.

“Human–AI Inquiry and the Topology of Epistemic Dependence.”

Models AI systems as locally adapting features of a community’s information infrastructure, and studies how the resulting dynamics affect the community’s resilience and plasticity.
